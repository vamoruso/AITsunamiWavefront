## 📘 About the Book

**AI Wavefront** is not a technical manual.  
It is a narrative exploration of the greatest transformation of our time:  
how AI is reshaping the way we think, create, work, love, trust, and hope.

Across 27 short, intense chapters, the book examines:

- how AI influences human behavior  
- how it transforms creativity and work  
- how it affects relationships and identity  
- how it redefines truth, trust, and autonomy  
- how we can remain human in a world accelerating beyond us  

Based on real studies and over **200,000 AI–human interactions**,  
AI Wavefront offers a clear, accessible, and deeply human perspective on the future.
